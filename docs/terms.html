1. 서비스 개요

Malsori(말소리, 이하 "앱")는 한국어능력시험(TOPIK)을 준비하는 외국인 학습자가 한글과 한국어 어휘, 실제 발음을 함께 익히도록 독립적으로 개발된 iOS 애플리케이션입니다. 앱은 한국어능력시험을 주관하는 국립국제교육원과 무관합니다. 앱은 한글 자모와 발음 규칙 안내, 그리고 1급부터 6급까지 여섯 레벨의 단어(전체 60권, 1권 = 100단어)를 제공하며, 각 레벨의 1권은 무료로, 나머지 권은 앱 내 구매(이용권)를 통해 이용할 수 있습니다.

1. About the service

Malsori (말소리, "the App") is an independently developed iOS application that helps non-Korean speakers preparing for the Test of Proficiency in Korean (TOPIK) learn Hangul, Korean vocabulary and how words are really pronounced. The App is not affiliated with the National Institute for International Education, which administers TOPIK. The App provides a guide to the Hangul letters and pronunciation rules, and vocabulary for six levels, 1 to 6 (60 books in all, 100 words per book). Book 1 of every level is free; the other books are available through an in-app purchase (a pass).

2. Free use and in-app purchases

  • Free: all of Book 1 (100 words) of every level and the whole Hangul guide (letters, final consonants, sound rules). Every feature works without limit inside the free range; no feature is sold separately.
  • Paid: Book 2 onward needs a pass. A pass is a yearly subscription, a monthly subscription or a lifetime pass; any of them opens every book of all six levels (60 books). There is no free trial.
  • The scope of a pass you have already bought is never reduced.
  • Auto-renewing subscriptions (monthly, yearly): unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, a subscription renews for the same period at the same price, and your Apple account is charged within 24 hours before the period ends. You can cancel at any time in iOS Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions, and you keep access until the paid period ends. Deleting the App does not cancel a subscription.
  • Lifetime pass: a one-time, non-consumable purchase. It does not renew and there are no further charges.
  • Price changes: subscription price changes are announced in advance under Apple's policies, and an increase is not applied to existing subscribers without their consent.
  • Prices are those shown in the App Store and may vary by country, exchange rate and promotion.
  • Payment is handled through your Apple account; no sign-up or login is required.
  • Purchases can be restored with "Restore purchases" on another device signed in to the same Apple account, or after reinstalling the App.
  • Refunds, including subscription refunds, follow the Apple App Store's refund policy. The Developer cannot process refunds directly; request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

3. Study data

  • The level you chose and your per-word study record are stored on your device and in your own iCloud account (key-value store).
  • The Developer does not collect or store your study data on any server.
  • Running "Start over" in the App clears the whole study record and cannot be undone.

4. Content and voice

  • Every word, example sentence and recording ships inside the App, and studying needs no internet connection. Content may be added or revised through App updates.
  • The voice that reads words, example sentences and meanings was not recorded by a person: it is pre-rendered with AI voice models. Hangul letters and a few lines are read by the voice built into the device.
  • The real pronunciation shown on a card is computed by the App by applying the rules of Standard Korean Pronunciation to the spelling. Rules that can only be decided with grammatical information are not applied, so for some words the pronunciation shown may differ from actual usage, or a sound change may not be shown.

  • All study content is original and contains no actual test questions.
