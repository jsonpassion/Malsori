FAQ

I can't read Hangul at all. Is this for me?

Yes. The first screen asks, and if you say no you land on the letters: consonants, vowels, how they stack into a block, and the seven sounds a final consonant can make. Every letter reads itself when pressed.

How does the app know how a word is pronounced?

It applies the automatic rules of Standard Korean Pronunciation to the spelling — the same engine as the demo above. Rules that need grammar to decide are left out on purpose: a wrong reading would teach you an error.

Is the voice a real person?

No. The Korean and English recordings are pre-rendered with AI voice models and bundled in the app, so they play instantly with no internet. Single letters are read by your device's own voice.

Do I need an account? What do you collect?

No account, no ads, no tracking, no analytics. Your progress stays on your device and in your own iCloud. See the privacy policy.
